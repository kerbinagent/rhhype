## Collateral, funding, and session

The fresh HL native and xyz responses both have `collateralToken=0`; [HL's contract specifications](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/contract-specifications) identify its perp margin as **USDC**. RH's [official deposit guide archived in this workspace](../data/raw/comparators/rh_lighter/20260929T040726Z/sources/deposits-transfers-and-withdrawals.md) says **USDG** is the asset accepted for direct deposits to its perp account. The fresh RH `assetDetails` response lists USDG (asset ID 3) as margin-enabled, while also listing some non-USDG assets with margin modes; it is not enough by itself to infer account-level collateral eligibility. Keep separate RH USDG and HL USDC balances and treat combined dollar reporting as conditional on parity, with no automatic transfer or conversion. [Robinhood's Lighter-domain documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/lighter-domains/) confirms this is a separate sequencer and liquidity domain from Lighter Core.

[HL documents hourly funding payments](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding). An earlier [RH public funding-history response](../data/raw/comparators/rh_lighter/20260929T040726Z/history/fundings_30d/NVDA.json) advertises `resolution: "1h"` and includes signed `direction`, but the three fresh market metadata responses do **not** contain current settled funding cash, a guaranteed next settlement, or an account-specific payment. The passive-exit replay must preserve settlement-crossing uncertainty and actual venue inventory; it cannot count unobserved funding as zero or transfer a rate from one venue to the other.

At 02:09 UTC on 30 September, New York local time was **22:09 EDT on 29 September**, outside the [Nasdaq regular 09:30–16:00 ET session and its stated after-hours period ending 20:00 ET](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity). NVDA's equity reference can therefore behave differently from a regular-session tape; `active` perp status does not imply regular-session liquidity or a continuously refreshed stock oracle. BTC/ETH are distinct crypto controls, and XAG is a metal reference rather than a cash equity. The study should retain asset/session labels, book age, spread, depth, oracle changes, and censor reasons instead of pooling these regimes. No public market-hours page reviewed here establishes a guaranteed RH or HIP-3 perp order-book pause or uninterrupted overnight execution.
