# Maker-flow round trip: exploratory 18:23 UTC archive

This applies the later [frozen method](../reports/maker-roundtrip-v1/frozen-method.md) to an archive collected **before** that method was fixed. It is exploratory, not a prospective validation. The [replay script](../scripts/analyze_maker_roundtrip.py) and [derived case ledger](../data/derived/maker-roundtrip-20260929T1823Z/cases.json) preserve every qualifying hypothetical full-flow event, including missing hedge and exit outcomes. No order was submitted or maker fill observed.

The primary policy assumes maker arrival after 1 second, tests BTC and ETH in both directions across HL↔Core and HL↔RH Lighter, and uses non-overlapping 5-second decision anchors. A full-flow signal requires prospective opposite-aggressor trade volume strictly greater than the displayed top size ahead **plus the full ~$1,000 base quantity**. At the first such signal, it selects the first time-valid hedge quote after 100 ms, then the first time-valid paired exit books 10 seconds after the hedge. A shallow first quote or pair is censored rather than replaced by later favorable depth. The 0.5- and 3-second arrival checks share observations with the primary policy.

Of 27 full-flow signals, the first hedge quote was too shallow in eight cases. Of 19 with a full hedge quote, the first eligible exit pair was too shallow in seven. These 15 cases are **missing conditional round-trip outcomes**, not zero-return trades or unentered positions; the eight without a hedge would be unresolved hypothetical unhedged exposures. The 12 complete displayed paths ranged from about **−13.55 to −7.02 bp** net after four own-notional fees. The separate 5 bp reserve on the larger entry notional made each result more negative. These paths are a selected subset with observed full depth and cannot estimate the average result of the 27 full-flow signals.

The predeclared decision-time entry-positive subset had two primary full-flow signals, both HL maker-sell ETH → RH hedge. Both had complete books yet returned **−7.97 and −9.39 bp** conditionally after fees; the 5 bp reserve sensitivities were **−12.97 and −14.39 bp**. The favorable opening quote had not captured the subsequent hedge and closing basis. No HL → Core entry-positive opening produced a primary full-flow signal. The exact [48 cohort gate ledgers](../data/derived/maker-roundtrip-20260929T1823Z/summary.json) reconcile supported quotes, arrival status, full-flow status, hedge status, and exit status for all cases and for the entry-positive subset. The 0.5- and 3-second controls had 17 and 3 complete paths, also zero positive after four fees; they overlap the primary cases and cannot be combined into portfolio P&L.

The 18:23 archive was capped after 394 seconds, rather than a full 10 minutes. It had one feed generation per venue, no recorded invalidation, and zero retained trade source timestamps ahead of local receipt in all six venue/asset streams. Clocks were not calibrated. The replay's 100 ms hedge quote search **does not model the [RH Lighter Standard tier's published 300 ms taker processing delay](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/docs/account-types)**, network-to-order latency, or exit order matching. That makes the conditional quote path optimistic as an execution model. Public trades cannot reveal a hypothetical ALO acknowledgement, queue position, hidden depth, cancellations, or an actual fill. Even a positive displayed conditional result would need separate delay- and fee-tier-aware testing before any execution inference.
