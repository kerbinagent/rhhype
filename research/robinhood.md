# Robinhood Chain venue research — 2026-09-29 UTC

This notebook records read-only observations for a Robinhood Chain versus Hyperliquid price comparison. It does **not** identify a risk-free arbitrage: the instruments, venues, jurisdictions and settlement paths differ. Re-run `python scripts/robinhood_probe.py` before drawing a live conclusion. ## What exists on mainnet

Robinhood [announced public mainnet on July 1, 2026](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/?lang=en), with Uniswap as its public AMM partner and Pleiades as a proprietary AMM partner. Its [network documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/connecting/) gives chain ID **4663**, public RPC `https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com`, and Blockscout `https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com`; testnet is **46630**. A live `eth_chainId` call returned `0x1237` (=4663), and the 03:51:18 UTC [saved snapshot](../data/raw/robinhood_snapshot_20260929T035118Z.json) read block **75,348,455**, timestamp 03:51:17 UTC. The public RPC is rate limited; Robinhood recommends an [archive provider for historical state reads](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/connecting/). Indeed, `uiMultiplier()` at a block about 28 hours old returned `historical state ... is not available` from the public RPC.

Robinhood's [canonical contract registry](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/contracts/) warns that a ticker-matching contract at another address is not its Stock Token. We fetched the issuer's documented [`/rhj/assets` API](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/) on 2026-09-29 UTC: **195** chain-4663 deployments. The registry is the address authority here. In particular, the official ticker is **GOOGL**, not GOOG, and **HOOD is absent**; a chain token using those names alone would not qualify. Classic Stock Tokens in the Robinhood Europe brokerage app are a separate earlier product from these onchain Stock Tokens, per [Robinhood's launch announcement](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/?lang=en).

## What a token holder owns and can do

The issuer [describes Stock Tokens](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj/faq/) as Jersey-issued **tokenized debt securities** backed by corresponding shares at a US custodian. Holders get economic exposure, but no legal or beneficial rights against the underlying equity issuer. The [chain documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/) says these are 18-decimal ERC-20 tokens and can be held, transferred and composed onchain. US persons, among other restricted jurisdictions, cannot be offered, sold or delivered the products. Any strategy must establish its own eligibility; an open AMM contract does not remove that restriction.

The `currentMultiplier` is **underlying shares per one raw token**. Robinhood [explains](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj/faq/) that cash dividends are reinvested into more underlying shares, increasing this multiplier instead of paying token holders cash. Splits also adjust it; raw onchain balances stay unchanged, and the [oracle price](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/) incorporates the multiplier. The issuer's [`/rhj/prices/{symbol}` API](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/) reports underlying-equity `bid`/`ask` **without** the multiplier; the live response also supplied token-adjusted `tokenBid`/`tokenAsk`. For a 1-token long versus a share-denominated hedge, hedge `currentMultiplier` shares, and recheck after corporate actions. For example, our AAPL snapshot multiplier was `1.000566080061092436`, rather than exactly one.

Primary creation is gated: [Robinhood's developer documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/) says only KYB-onboarded Authorised Participants could subscribe from the issuer at launch (BBVI was the sole one). The [issuer FAQ](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj/faq/) says holders may sell on a secondary venue or, where there is no Authorised Participant, redeem directly with the issuer subject to KYC/AML. This is not an unconditional instant convertibility path. Its [product page](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj/product/) states a 0% investor redemption fee for the first 90 days after issue, then 0.05%, subject to terms that may change. The [tokenization window](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/) for market-maker mint/burn is Monday 02:00 CET/CEST to Saturday 02:00 CET/CEST; secondary swaps can occur outside it. Per-asset session capability is exposed in `/rhj/assets`.

## Historical access and limitations

GeckoTerminal's public network slug is **`robinhood`**. Its [NVDA/USDG pool endpoint](https://api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2/networks/robinhood/pools/0xd4EB21209C4D6093f80B5b84f5C45cc093EA14a3) and [hourly OHLCV endpoint](https://api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2/networks/robinhood/pools/0xd4EB21209C4D6093f80B5b84f5C45cc093EA14a3/ohlcv/hour?aggregate=1&limit=1000&currency=usd&token=base) returned data on 2026-09-29 UTC. The [saved hourly history](../data/raw/robinhood_history_complete_20260929T040036Z.json) covers AAPL, NVDA, GOOGL, MSFT, TSLA, AMZN, META, QQQ and SPY v3 USDG pools, with 782–1,000 bars per pool. GeckoTerminal initially returned HTTP 429 after five pool requests, so four were collected after its rate window reset. Robinhood's [corporate-action feed](https://api.robinhood.com/rhj/corporate-actions) exposed 50 recent events, not a complete history. The public RPC could not read historical *state* 28 hours back, but did return ERC-8056 `UIMultiplierUpdated` and cancellation *logs* in chunks of at most ten million blocks. The [saved multiplier timeline](../data/raw/robinhood_multipliers_20260929T040952Z.json) scans nine canonical contracts from block 35,000,000 (2026-08-13, before the 30-day comparison window) through block 75,359,513. It found seven updates and no cancellations; each update's prior multiplier was exactly 1.0, and the last effective new multiplier exactly matched the live registry. AAPL changed Aug 14, NVDA Sep 10, GOOGL Sep 15, MSFT Sep 11, META Sep 21, QQQ Sep 22 and SPY Sep 18 (UTC); TSLA and AMZN stayed 1.0. Historical candles are prices **per token**, so divide each bar by the multiplier **effective at that bar's timestamp** before a per-share comparison. At the transition hour, an OHLC bar can straddle the update, so avoid that bar or process subhour data. Candles still do not reconstruct actual bid/ask or depth.

**Do not use GeckoTerminal's `currency=usd` Robinhood candle values for historical basis without cross-checking.** At 2026-09-23 13:00 UTC its AMZN/USDG USD value was about 1.056 times its same-pool `currency=token` (USDG) value. This is an indexer USDG/USD conversion anomaly: [independent CoinGecko hourly USDG/USD observations](../data/raw/robinhood_usdg_coingecko_20260929T041551Z.json) put USDG at **$0.999946** that hour, and between $0.999787 and $1.000091 across Sep 21–26. Paxos [documents 1:1 USD redemption](https://docs.sandbox.paxos.com/guides/stablecoin/usdg/index), subject to its eligibility and operational terms. For a historical pool-versus-perp diagnostic, request GeckoTerminal candles with `currency=token&token=base` to retain **USDG per Stock Token**, then apply an independent USDG/USD series and the historical multiplier. CoinGecko's global price is indicative and does not prove an executable USDG conversion on Robinhood Chain.

## Interpretation for a cross-venue study

Compare only exact underliers and quote units. A Robinhood Stock Token is a cash-dividend-reinvesting claim on `multiplier` shares with Jersey issuer and custody risk. A Hyperliquid equity perpetual, if available, has funding, margin, liquidation and market-basis risks; it is not the same deliverable security. `GOOGL` should not silently match `GOOG`; fund tokens such as GLD, SGOV and USO should not be paired to a generic gold, interest-rate or crude future on name alone. `SPCX` does refer to the currently public Space Exploration Technologies Corp. Class A stock: its [SEC June 2026 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026052535/spcx-20260630.htm) reports an IPO that month, so it should not be treated as a still-private security. To test a candidate, take near-simultaneous **executable-size** buy and sell quotes on both venues, normalize the Stock Token multiplier, then subtract AMM/venue fees, gas, stablecoin conversion, bridge and funding costs. The price differences above are observations, not trade instructions.
