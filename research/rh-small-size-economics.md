# RH maker size economics: keep $1,000 primary

**Decision memo, 29 September 2026.** The existing stopped-archive evidence supports keeping **$1,000 as the primary RH maker → Hyperliquid (HL) hedge paper branch**, with $100, $250, and $500 as size sensitivities. It does not justify adding a branch below $100 or lowering the **$0.10 net per completed episode** target. These are research thresholds, not a live trading recommendation. This memo uses only earlier stopped studies and does not inspect the current maker holdout.

## What the arithmetic requires

The [frozen pilot inputs](../reports/rh-small-maker-v1/assumptions.json) use RH Lighter **Standard** (0 bp maker entry, 0 bp taker exit), two HL **taker** fills (4.5 bp each for native BTC/ETH; 0.9 bp each for sampled `xyz:NVDA`/`xyz:SILVER`), and a **separate modeled 5 bp reserve** on the larger entry notional. The archived [official RH account schedule](../data/raw/comparators/rh_lighter/20260929T040726Z/sources/account-types.md), [RH Standard metadata](../reports/rh-small-maker-v1/policy-metadata-standard.json), [RH Premium sensitivity metadata](../reports/rh-small-maker-v1/policy-metadata-premium.json), and [HL fee schedule](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) distinguish those public rates; they are not an authenticated user's account quote. RH Premium base would add roughly **1.2 bp maker + 3.5 bp exit taker** on RH's own two leg notionals, while changing timing. It cannot be evaluated by fee subtraction with Standard's unchanged fills.

The table is an **approximately budget-sized** round-trip hurdle. The actual ledger charges every fee on its **own executed leg notional**, and reserve once on `max(RH entry, HL entry)`. It therefore uses actual quantity and price rather than the nominal budget. The displayed hurdle is the gross four-price cash-flow edge needed to clear fees, reserve, and the fixed $0.10 target, before capital, funding, or conversion. It is not an opening-spread threshold: the four executed prices must also reflect the closing basis and execution price changes. Those price effects must not be subtracted twice. Failed or partial hedges require their actual cash flows and residual inventory rather than this equal-notional approximation.

The **$0.10 target is fixed in dollars**, so its percentage burden is ten times larger at $100 than at $1,000. Published trading fees, the modeled reserve, and the pilot's capital charge scale approximately with *actual* notionals (capital also with exposure time). Venue lot and minimum-order rules create discontinuities: quantity is floored to a lot valid on both venues, never upsized, and each submitted order must pass its own minimum. In the [same-anchor stopped-book comparison](../reports/rh-hedge-venue-choice/matched-rows.csv), all four sampled assets had valid $100 orders, but median RH entry notionals were about **$99.31 BTC, $99.88 ETH, $99.83 NVDA, and $99.55 XAG** rather than exactly $100. These observed floors do not prove that still smaller orders will be valid, hedgeable, or closable after partial fills. Any fixed transfer, conversion, infrastructure, or operational cost remains unmeasured and would weigh more heavily on smaller attempts; no such cost is invented in the table.

## What the stopped market evidence says

- The [matched HL versus Lighter Core book study](rh-hedge-venue-choice.md) prices the same original quantity and future unwind at **$100/$250/$500/$1,000**. At $1,000, median HL net after its actual modeled fees and the 5 bp reserve was **−$1.425 BTC, −$1.471 ETH, −$0.739 NVDA, and −$0.704 XAG**. No matched $1,000 path reached +$0.10. Core Standard's zero explicit hedge fee lowered the hurdle, especially for crypto, but all four median Core paths also remained negative. None of the smaller-size median Core paths became positive after reserve. These are *static book paths conditional on a hypothetical RH maker fill*, not an execution or fill-rate estimate.
- The separate [RH bid/ask quote-distance flow ladder](../reports/rh-quote-distance-flow/REPORT.md) kept identical anchors and examined $100/$250/$500/$1,000. A smaller order sometimes raises the **possible full-flow** count at the best price: BTC best ask had 13/34 complete windows at $100 versus 5/34 at $1,000. That is potential public-flow attribution after displayed queue ahead, not a private fill. Across BTC, ETH, NVDA, and XAG, **no complete 10-second window at 5, 10, or 20 bp behind the best had an at/through print**. Shifting a small quote far enough to offset its higher target percentage therefore had no observed flow in these brief archives. A rare deep sweep could have a different adverse hedge markout; best-price calibration must not be transferred to it.
- In the earlier [prospective maker round-trip capture](maker-roundtrip-prospective.md), 32 primary hypothetical full-flow signals left only 14 complete displayed hedge-and-unwind paths, all negative. On those unchanged complete paths, deleting *all* trading fees left a maximum positive gross of **$0.052712**, below $0.10; nine hedge and nine exit paths remained unresolved. This supports caution about lowering size or fees as a complete fix, but it is not a size-stratified RH pilot result. The unrelated [frozen taker holdout fee sensitivity](../reports/fee-sensitivity/REPORT.md) likewise stayed negative with both fees and the reserve deleted; its fill process differs from maker quoting and must not be read as a maker return.

## Size and target decision

Keep the four already specified branches and **$1,000 primary**. Smaller nominal size can reduce dollars at risk and may make a full public-flow signal more common, but it also makes the fixed $0.10 net target materially harder in basis points. The stopped studies do not show a completed, realizable, all-cost small-size edge. Keep the target as *net of all four leg fees, reserve, and applicable capital/funding items*, rather than relabeling a pre-cost quote edge as profit.

Going below $100 would require new, predeclared evidence that the exact lot-rounded order and **each hedge/exit increment** meet venue constraints, that queue-attributed flow occurs often enough at the **economically required quote price**, and that the first available hedge and both exits produce a positive all-cost conditional distribution with adequate coverage and explicit unresolved inventory. Changing the absolute $0.10 target would require a reason tied to measured fixed operating costs, capital usage, risk per attempt, and a fresh chronological holdout; merely lowering it to make a negative or no-flow cell appear acceptable would not establish an edge. Neither change is warranted by the stopped archives alone.
