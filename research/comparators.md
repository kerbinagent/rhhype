## Funding and fees

- Lighter `/fundings` timestamps are **seconds**, while its candle `t` is **milliseconds**. Historical `rate` is a **percentage per one-hour event**: `0.0012` means 0.0012%, or 0.000012 of notional. The `direction` field identifies the payer: `long` makes longs pay shorts; `short` makes shorts pay longs. Its `value` field cross-checks this scale (`~$0.938` on one BTC at ~$78,140). Do not mix this with `/funding-rates` without independent scaling; that endpoint also includes other venues' rows. Lighter first capped hourly candles at 500; an older window was fetched and the stored series merged to 720 hours.
- Aster `fundingRate` is a signed **fraction per settlement event** with `fundingTime` in milliseconds. In these 30 days BTC/ETH/SOL/SPY had about 90 events (8-hour cadence), HYPE/XAU about 180 (4-hour cadence), and NVDA 94 (variable). Normalize to cash flows at actual event times; do not blindly multiply an event rate by 3 or 8.
- dYdX historical `rate` is a signed **fraction per hourly event**, `effectiveAt` is ISO time. The public endpoint returned 730 latest rows for a 730-row request. Restrict to the same 720-hour window as other venues before comparing.
- [Lighter account types](https://apidocs.lighter.xyz/docs/account-types): Standard default is 0% maker/taker, Plus 0.005% both, Premium at 0 LIT is 0.0040% maker / 0.0280% taker, with stake discounts. Premium differs in latency and quotas. [Aster current public schedule](https://docs.asterdex.com/trading/perpetuals/fees-and-specs/fees): USDT perps 0% maker / 0.04% taker; USD1 perps 0% / 0.005%, before VIP/ASTER discounts. The authenticated `commissionRate` endpoint is needed to know a particular account's actual fee. [GMX fees](https://docs.gmx.io/docs/trading/fees/) include side-dependent position fees, price impact, borrowing, funding, UI and network fees; gold and oil have different on/off-hours fee schedules.
- [Aster collateral modes](https://docs.asterdex.com/trading/perpetuals/single-asset-mode-and-multi-asset-mode) allow USDT-only single-asset margin or multi-asset cross margin with collateral haircuts; the quote symbol alone does not reveal the actual collateral funding or conversion cost. GMX market labels encode index and pool collateral separately, for example `ETH/USD [WETH-USDC]`.
- [dYdX trading fees](https://help.dydx.trade/en/articles/166995-trading-fees-on-dydx) depend on trailing 30-day account volume and can change through governance. The [public on-chain fee-tier query](https://dydx-ops-rest.kingnodes.com/dydxprotocol/v4/feetiers/perpetual_fee_params) returned base tier `taker_fee_ppm=500`, **5 bp**, and `maker_fee_ppm=100`, **1 bp** on 2026-09-29. The [per-market discount query](https://dydx-ops-rest.kingnodes.com/dydxprotocol/v4/feetiers/market_fee_discount_params) returned only expired entries. This verifies a baseline; an account with sufficient volume or other discounts can pay less. [dYdX funding documentation](https://help.dydx.trade/en/articles/166992-default-funding-rates-on-dydx) confirms hourly settlement and payer direction.
- [Lighter API limits](https://apidocs.lighter.xyz/docs/rate-limits) are 60 public requests per rolling minute for Standard/unauthenticated use; HTTP 405 **can mean rate limited** as well as HTTP 429. A firewall cooldown can last 60 seconds. Plan broad universes with market rotation and leave headroom for metadata rather than assuming unlimited books.

For a pair, gross executable entry edge should be computed from **same-time, size-matched** bid on the short venue and ask on the long venue. Deduct taker/maker fees on entry and exit, expected exit spread/impact, one-time transfer or bridge costs if relevant, and the signed funding/borrowing cash flows for the chosen holding period. Report each leg's margin currency and USD conversion separately. A 24h trade-volume figure cannot substitute for executable size; resting depth can disappear before either leg fills.

## Other popular venues and Robinhood stock tokens

### Robinhood Chain Lighter domain (separate liquidity)

[Robinhood Chain's Lighter domain](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/lighter-domains/) has its own sequencer and liquidity, with public API `https://api.rh.lighter.xyz/api/v1/`. It must not be pooled with Lighter Core. [Robinhood Chain's EVM chain ID](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/connecting/) is **4663**; [RH Lighter's application chain ID](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/docs/get-started) is **466324**, which is a separate identifier. The [RH Lighter API](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/reference/orderbookdetails) showed 57 active perpetual and 27 active spot markets on 2026-09-29; the raw inventory is `data/raw/comparators/rh_lighter/20260929T040726Z/markets.json`. A paired 20-minute collection of 22 perps and 8 stock-token/USDG spot books sampled RH and Hyperliquid sequentially for each market, retaining request/receipt timestamps and Hyperliquid's engine timestamp in `paired_books.jsonl`. All 600 RH and 520 mapped Hyperliquid book requests succeeded. The 520 same-pair receipt offsets had median 203 ms, 95th percentile 291 ms, maximum 757 ms; Hyperliquid's engine age had median 596 ms and 95th percentile 819 ms. This same run's `history/` contains 720 hourly candles and 720 hourly funding events for BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, XAU, XAG, SPY, NVDA, AAPL, META, and MSFT, plus 720 hourly candles for SPY/USDG and NVDA/USDG spot; 37 paced public history calls returned successfully.

The initial RH book's smaller-side 10 bp depth was about $1.65m BTC, $0.92m ETH, $0.34m SOL, $0.27m HYPE, $0.47m XAU, $0.23m NVDA perp, and $0.58m SPY perp. RH's **SPY/USDG token spot** had about $59k and NVDA/USDG about $27k within 10 bp, despite SPY/USDG reporting $20.6m of 24-hour volume. These are returned-book snapshots (at most 250 individual orders per side), not guaranteed fills. Like any spot versus perp strategy, stock-token spot cannot be shorted merely by selling an unowned token, and inventory/collateral mobility changes the trade.

[RH Lighter account types](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/docs/account-types) set default Standard maker/taker at **0%/0%**; Plus at **0.005%/0.005%**; Premium base at **0.012% maker / 0.035% taker** below $1m trailing 14-day volume, falling with volume. Standard's stated taker latency is 300 ms; Premium's base tier is 200 ms with immediate maker/cancel. This RH Premium baseline is 3.5 bp taker, different from Core's 2.8 bp. RH `orderBookDetails` metadata showed `0.0000` maker/taker in the sampled markets, consistent with Standard, but actual account tier and execution fee must be checked before a trade. [RH API rate limits](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/docs/rate-limits) are 60 requests per rolling minute for Standard public reads; both HTTP 405 and 429 can mean throttling. Official fee/rate docs are archived in this run's `sources/` directory.

For RH stock spot identity, `/assetDetails` returned canonical Robinhood Chain (`chainId=4663`) token contract addresses and multipliers matching the [issuer's asset registry](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/). The [RH Lighter orderBooks definition](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/reference/orderbooks) defines `min_base_amount` as a **base-token** amount, and orders report `remaining_base_amount`. Thus the book quantity is a token quantity. The `multiplier` is shares per token (e.g. SPY 1.001718, NVDA 1.000775); it is needed when hedging against an underlying share-perp or equity quote. A contemporaneous SPY Lighter token book midpoint of $764.73 and RH Lighter index price of $764.96 were closer to the issuer's multiplier-adjusted token midpoint $764.94 than its raw underlying midpoint $763.63. NVDA Lighter index $228.437 similarly matched the issuer token midpoint $228.452 more closely than its raw underlying midpoint $228.275. This supports treating spot price as USD per token, but the API definition does not explicitly state this price convention. The raw validation (`spot_unit_validation.json`) and issuer quote times are archived. `spot_registry_enrichment.json` separately links the eight spot markets to canonical token contracts without changing the original paired book rows. Spot-versus-perp edge should remain a sensitivity range until the price convention is independently confirmed. [RH Lighter's deposit guide](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/docs/deposits-transfers-and-withdrawals) explicitly says **only USDG can be deposited to its perps account**; its spot account has a separate deposit route. RH USDG perp/spot balances, HL USDC margin and any bridge/FX cost are separate cash flows.

### What the archive supports

It supports an initial screen, not a backtested arbitrage profit claim. The 30-day candle/funding panels can test basis/funding behavior; the live JSONL panels can test spread/depth persistence. A credible realized-profit estimate still needs entry and exit order-book walks at synchronized timestamps, fee tier, partial-fill and hedge-delay assumptions, contract matching, borrowing/funding, and the ability to trade in each relevant jurisdiction and account. The collector does not trade or touch balances.
