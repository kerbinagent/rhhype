## Question and interpretation

Can an original fixed quantity obtain a target-positive **observed paired-book quote outcome** when both Core/RH entries are observed after a fixed 500 ms delay and both exits after a ten-second hold plus the same delay? Keep public fees, separate 5 bp stress, capital and the $0.10 target unchanged.

The [earlier Core comparison](passive-hedge-venue-followup.md) assumes RH maker entry at the anchor and RH maker exit at the first matched +10–16 s quote. All stressed outcomes were negative. Crossing RH's spread at those same timestamps only worsens that bound. A delayed entry changes the price path and is untested. This route/timing gap supplies a falsifiable question, without supplying evidence of favorable economics. The recent RH-only static upper bound does not bound future cross-venue basis movement.

The completed [RH/HL delayed study](../reports/delayed-taker-quotes/0252Z-v3-analysis.md) found no positive covered outcome after fees alone. Improved basis forecast MAE previously failed the fixed-quantity economic hurdle. These are strong objections to fitting another predictor before establishing positive economic labels. This study fits no predictor and selects no asset, size, horizon, entry timestamp or delay from outcomes. Seven-minute archives cannot establish a stable convergence process. Direct venue-profit comparisons with the later 0252Z archive are invalid because capture windows differ.

The paired observational clock omits leg-specific order arrival/fills, limit-price rejection, partial execution and hedge rescue. Public 300 ms Standard taker processing is contextual information, not private arrival evidence. The fixed 500 ms clock follows the prior diagnostic and is not tuned to that lower bound. Outcomes are conditional quote arithmetic, never actual inventory, realized P&L, executable return, fill probability or an all-admitted execution claim. USDG/USDC parity and common linear base exposure are explicit assumptions; conversion and collateral are unmeasured.

## Fixed candidate grid and quantity

For each archive use its exact manifest start `T`, its two assets and both directions: long RH/short Core and long Core/short RH. Primary $1,000 anchors are `T+5k seconds`, `k=0..83`. Diagnostic $100/$250/$500 anchors are `T+30k seconds`, `k=0..13` for each size. This gives **4 assets ×2 directions ×(84+3×14) =1,008 candidates**, comprising **672 primary /336 smaller-size candidates, 504 per archive**, with one ten-second outcome each. No warmup, washout, future coverage or positive static gate removes a candidate. Anchor IDs deterministically include archive/asset/direction/budget/k. Initial invalid anchors and all terminal tails remain.

At an anchor consume the complete receipt-tied batch first, then select the current paired books. Both must be valid, uncrossed, same-generation reconstructed states with receipt and source ages **≤2 s**, source≤receipt, and paired source/receipt skew **≤1 s**, matching RH/HL v3. Freeze `q=floor(B / long-venue best ask / common lot)×common lot`, using the exact rational least common multiple of the two known lot steps. Check both venues' known minima and **walk both entry-direction anchor legs** (long ask and short bid) at q, matching the prior RH/HL anchor rule. A shallow anchor leg or known anchor rule failure terminates that candidate with null economics; unknown historical rules remain unknown. Missing/invalid anchor leaves q and economics null. Keep the anchor references, q and both full-depth anchor leg notionals. Budget is a sizing reference: depth already can exceed it, and delayed prices can drift. Report both effects; do not reject excess or resize q. Every entry/exit walk uses that same q. No positive static/economic anchor gate is added.

## First paired quotes, ties, gaps and EOF

Entry request is the anchor; entry due is anchor +500 ms and the inclusive hard deadline is anchor +2 s. The first paired state at/after due must satisfy the same validity, 2 s receipt/source freshness and 1 s paired skew limits, with each venue's receipt/source timestamp at least due and greater than its anchor references. Each selected book must remain in the anchor generation. Select the first time/book-eligible pair before checking depth and known rule legality; a shallow/known-illegal pair is a terminal failure, with no later retry.

Shared entry receipt is that pair's observation receipt. Exit request is shared entry receipt +10 s; exit due is request +500 ms and its inclusive deadline is request +2 s. Each exit receipt/source must be at least due and advance beyond its entry references, in the same generation. Apply the same first-pair/known-rule/depth rule. Ten seconds is measured from shared paired entry receipt; there is no best future timestamp or alternate horizon.

Process canonical records in nondecreasing receipt order; reject missing/noninteger/regressing receipts and identity conflicts. Apply a complete equal-receipt batch before activating/evaluating due windows and before an equal-deadline timeout. A pair at due, deadline or cutoff may qualify in that batch. Invalidation has effect throughout the batch: a genuine source/nonce/generation/book failure censors affected unresolved candidates even if a later update in that batch is fresh. Do not erase a real cutoff gap.

Any explicit affected-venue/source/generation/nonce/book invalidation after an anchor through its exit censors that unresolved candidate; valid endpoints do not imply continuous holding coverage. Gap censorship is permanent only for that candidate's lifecycle. A later fixed anchor may start with a new valid same-generation snapshot/baseline, including a new connection generation, and remains in the original grid; it does not inherit an unrelated retired candidate's halt. Failed snapshot establishment, nonce gap, source regression/future clock, connection error, unexpected close, empty/crossed or overflowing book cannot be repaired by retaining the old state. Freshness expiry is an entry/exit eligibility failure, not proof of disconnect or an inferred intermediate book gap. Ticker/trade freshness never repairs a book.

Cutoff is exactly `T+420 s`, irrespective of the slightly later manifest end. At that instant handle genuine data evidence and eligible pairs before EOF. Declared final shutdown controls corresponding to that manifest's recorded terminal generation close are planned closures, not unexpected gaps; suppress only their EOF-censor effect at/after cutoff. Earlier real gaps/deadline failures keep their status. Remaining entry/exit windows are `entry_eof`/`exit_eof`; do not liquidate at close. Continue decoding after cutoff solely to verify terminal records and gzip integrity, producing no new quote outcome.

## Four-leg accounting and summaries

For fixed q let A be entry long-buy walk, B entry short-sell, C exit long-sell and D exit short-buy. `gross=B−A+C−D`. Each leg fee is its own actual notional ×its venue's frozen public Standard taker rate /10,000. `fee_only_net=gross−four_fees`. `stress=max(A,B)×0.0005`. `capital=(A+B)×0.05×(exit_pair_receipt−entry_pair_receipt)/(365×86400 s)`. `adjusted_quote_net_ex_funding=fee_only_net−stress−capital`. No capital reset or extra favorable fee sensitivity.

Funding-inclusive P&L is unknown on every row. Report UTC-hour crossing/tie flags using the actual paired entry/exit interval; absence of a crossing does not establish a verified zero funding payment. Unknown conversion, borrowing, liquidation and collateral remain disclosed rather than assigned zero execution cost.

Their global denominators are **264 /240 /264 /240**; do not equalize them or discard boundary anchors. Report timing/structural/depth numeric quote coverage separately from historical order-legality coverage: a complete numeric quote can have unknown legality, and any missing rule leaves the conjunction unknown.
