# Maker-first, taker-hedge feasibility

**Decision:** The available public feeds can support a conservative *candidate and markout study*. They cannot establish where an unplaced maker order would sit in the queue, whether it would fill, or expected realized maker-plus-hedge profit. A touched quote is not a fill. No orders, credentials, or monitor changes are part of this proposal.

## What is observable

| Venue | Public quote and trade evidence | Queue-position limit |

A 15-second, read-only BTC feed check on 2026-09-29 UTC acknowledged all three subscriptions on each venue, without errors: Hyperliquid **81 trades / 21 trade messages**, Lighter Core **120 / 46**, RH-domain Lighter **11 / 9**. The Lighter trade messages included maker-side and order-ID fields. This proves feed availability for the sampled BTC markets, not continuous coverage or execution quality. The current strategy monitor stores quote books and paper outcomes; it does not maintain synchronized public trade and order-level histories adequate to estimate queue dynamics.

## Fee and order semantics

Hyperliquid's [ALO/post-only order](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/exchange-endpoint) cancels when it would immediately cross; it does not guarantee a resting order will fill later. The [official fee schedule](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) has a **positive 1.5 bp tier-0 native perp maker fee** and **4.5 bp taker fee**. A maker *rebate* is conditional on qualifying maker volume and account/asset settings; none is assumed. HIP-3 deployer fee scale, growth mode, collateral alignment, and account tier change the result. Lighter [Core Standard](https://apidocs.lighter.xyz/docs/account-types) lists 0% maker and taker fees; Plus and Premium charge positive maker fees. The archived official [RH-domain account types](../data/raw/comparators/rh_lighter/20260929T040726Z/sources/account-types.md) likewise distinguish Standard from paid tiers. Fee fields in public market metadata are an assumption about the sampled tier, not an authenticated wallet quote. A shadow model should use a **fee cost**, never an unverified rebate.

## Conservative shadow experiment

Use only direct same-unit BTC and ETH perp pairs first: Hyperliquid native versus Lighter Core, with the RH-domain Lighter BTC/ETH books tracked separately because its USDG collateral adds conversion and margin costs. Freeze market mapping, fixed quote size such as **$1,000**, lot grids, quote price rule, minimum edge, and observation horizon *before* collecting. A share-price reference or stock token is outside this first test because it adds proxy and token-unit risk.

1. **Prospective quote.** At time `t0`, record both fresh books and a hypothetical post-only maker bid or ask that does not cross, at an exchange-valid tick and lot. Use the actual opposite-venue depth for the same base quantity. Record every candidate, including those that never fill. Compute the entry edge from maker limit price versus the *size-walked* opposite taker book, then subtract the maker fee, opposite taker fee, reserved exit costs, expected collateral conversion, and funding. An entry edge alone is not a round-trip P&L.
2. **Arrival and queue ahead.** Do not assume the quote arrives at `t0`. Re-evaluate after at least 0.5 s, 1 s, and 3 s hypothetical arrival delays (with broader delays if measured local order acknowledgements later warrant them). If the quote would cross then, mark **post-only reject**. Otherwise assign *all displayed size already at its price at arrival* ahead of the hypothetical order. For an inside-spread quote, this can be zero in the snapshot, but the arrival race remains unknown. Lighter's published Standard taker latency is 300 ms; its stated maker latency does not remove client, network, or matching delay. Hyperliquid's published colocated transaction latency is not a measured latency from this workspace.
3. **Trade-only queue test.** For a maker ask, count only subsequent aggressive buys at or through its price; for a maker bid, count only aggressive sells at or through its price. Require trade IDs and uninterrupted, correctly ordered quote/trade streams. Never credit a decrease in visible queue size that is not explained by an eligible trade: it may be a cancellation, hidden order event, or feed gap. Record when cumulative eligible trade quantity exceeds the conservative queue-ahead amount, and when it exceeds queue-ahead **plus proposed size**. Label these **possible partial/full-fill evidence**, not fills. Even a trade through the price is counterfactual after adding a new order: the extra displayed liquidity could alter subsequent taker behavior.
4. **Partial fill and hedge.** For each possible filled increment, simulate a taker hedge **after** the exchange trade time plus feed receipt delay and an explicit hedge submission delay. Use the first complete hedge book at or after that time, walking the same *partial* quantity; if depth is unavailable, classify the hedge cost as unknown or failed, never reuse an older favorable book. Cancel the unfilled maker remainder only after a modeled cancel delay, and retain worst-case unhedged inventory until the hedge is complete. Measure 0.5, 1, 3, and 10 second post-fill markouts on the hedge side to expose adverse selection.
5. **Report scenarios, not a win rate.** Report candidate count; post-only rejection; no-fill, possible-partial, and possible-full-fill counts; feed-gap exclusions; queue-ahead and wait distributions; maker-to-hedge price moves; hedge latency/depth failures; fees and collateral; and 10th/50th/90th-percentile conditional markouts. A **zero-fill** case gives no maker execution P&L. A permissive trade-through case is an **optimistic hypothetical execution scenario**, not an observed fill, estimated expected profit, or mathematical P&L bound. Include all aborted and unhedged cases rather than selecting only favorable completed pairs.

The paired economics for a possible maker **buy** followed by taker **short** hedge start with `hedge bid VWAP − maker buy price`; the reverse starts with `maker sell price − hedge ask VWAP`. Both use the same base quantity and are reduced by each leg's fee at its own notional, eventual exit costs, financing/funding, USDG↔USDC conversion when applicable, and unhedged price risk. Maker rebates enter only after an authenticated account fee tier is known.

## Minimal bounded public capture

Collect one prospective **10-minute** read-only session for BTC/ETH on Hyperliquid and Lighter Core, and optionally a separate RH-domain BTC/ETH session, with at most two markets per venue and a **25 MB total compressed** cap. Stop cleanly and record truncation if that cap is reached. Subscribe to Hyperliquid `bbo`, `l2Book`, and `trades`; Lighter `order_book/{id}`, `ticker/{id}`, and `trade/{id}`. Store receipt UTC, source timestamp, raw trade ID, side, price, size, and Lighter nonce/`begin_nonce`; save bounded snapshots of the first few levels and only candidate-adjacent order-level REST snapshots, paced within each venue's public limits. If a feed disconnects or nonce breaks, terminate that candidate and wait for a fresh snapshot. A manifest should state exact market IDs, stream generations, message counts, dropped messages, clock offset check, storage cap, and collection start/end. This capture is enough to test the shadow classifier and adverse-selection bounds. It is not enough to calibrate true fill probability without actual order acknowledgements and fills, which this research does not request.

## No-go conditions

- Treat the route as **unassessable** if either book is stale, source clocks cannot be aligned, Lighter `begin_nonce` breaks, a trade feed is missing, the maker quote's arrival/cancel state is ambiguous, or hedge depth at the modeled execution time is unavailable.
- Do not call a touching or disappearing top level a maker fill. Do not advance queue on unexplained size decreases. Do not assign a positive maker rebate from public market metadata.
- Do not infer positive expected P&L from a trade-through hypothetical scenario, especially when it depends on skipped partials, missed hedges, a single latency assumption, or an unmodeled exit. A shadow result cannot authorize live orders.
- Reject routes with non-equivalent contracts, unverified token/share units, inaccessible collateral, a required borrow/transfer path, or an account-tier fee that invalidates the edge. The RH-domain venue requires its own conversion and account checks.

The next decision after this capture is whether adverse selection and hedge delay leave any robust *conditional* edge worth calibrating. Real maker fill probability remains unidentified from public passive observation alone.


## Fee and matching mechanics recheck, September 29 18:39 UTC

The official [fee table](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees)
still lists native-perp base tier 0 at 4.5 bp taker and 1.5 bp maker. Replacing
one $1,000 taker leg with an actual maker fill saves $0.30 in this model;
that saving is separate from spread capture and adverse selection. HIP-3
market multipliers and collateral-specific rules must be applied separately.
The [aligned asset specification](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hypercore/aligned-quote-assets)
distinguishes AQAv1's fee benefit from AQAv2, which has no trading-fee benefit;
a blanket aligned-token discount would be incorrect.

[HyperCore matching](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hypercore/order-book)
uses price-time priority and groups cancel actions before GTC/IOC actions
inside each consensus batch. [ALO](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/order-types)
executes only as a resting order. Public receipt times and trade-through events
do not reconstruct a hypothetical order's acknowledgement, queue position,
or cancellation ordering. The first archive study therefore remains a
conditional public quote/trade diagnostic, with no maker fill probability or
realized return attributed to unplaced orders.
