# Proposed NVDA and silver maker-flow study

**Status: research proposal for root review. No capture, order, or production change has been launched for this study.** The [prospective BTC/ETH maker replay](maker-roundtrip-prospective.md) found zero positive four-fee conditional displayed round trips among 14 complete paths, but its native HL fees and crypto market behavior do not establish the result for HIP-3 stocks and metals. The proposal below uses the same queue-flow and first-book policy, with the missing Standard-tier taker processing delay added before any NVDA/XAG data are collected.

## Why the fee comparison changes

The [19:33 UTC frozen monitor metadata](../data/paper-monitor/markets.json) maps `xyz:NVDA` and `xyz:SILVER` to HL `xyz` markets. The [03:50 UTC HL asset metadata](../data/raw/hyperliquid/20260929T034903Z/meta_and_ctxs/xyz.json) records `deployerFeeScale="1.0"` and `growthMode="enabled"` **on each asset**, with USDC collateral token index 0. [Hyperliquid's published tier-0 perps rates and HIP-3 formula](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) give, before wallet-specific discounts or rebates:

| Fee at sampled settings | Native BTC/ETH | `xyz:NVDA` and `xyz:SILVER` |
|---|---:|---:|
| HL maker entry | 1.5 bp | **0.3 bp** = 1.5 × 2 × 0.1 |
| HL taker trade | 4.5 bp | **0.9 bp** = 4.5 × 2 × 0.1 |
| HL-maker + Lighter-hedge four-fee allowance | 6.0 bp | **1.2 bp** (0.3 entry maker + 0.9 HL exit taker) |
| Lighter-maker + HL-hedge four-fee allowance | 9.0 bp | **1.8 bp** (two 0.9 HL taker trades) |

The paired Core and RH Lighter Standard accounts list 0 bp maker/taker, with **300 ms taker processing latency** ([Core account types](https://apidocs.lighter.xyz/docs/account-types), [RH account types](https://apidocs.rh.lighter.xyz/docs/account-types)). The HL-maker fee saving against an HL taker entry is only **0.6 bp, or $0.06 per $1,000**, at these sampled HIP-3 settings; it must be kept separate from any spread capture. The crypto four-fee allowances were 4.8–7.2 bp higher depending on maker venue. This fee difference justifies a separate study, not a profitability claim. Recheck per-asset `deployerFeeScale`, `growthMode`, collateral alignment, account `userFees`, and Lighter account tier at the time of any future capture. A positive maker rate is a cost, not a rebate. The aligned-quote rule affects the protocol share according to the official formula; no blanket discount is assumed.

Use `Decimal`, not float, to compute `q = floor((1000 / maker_best_price) / common_step) × common_step`; require the *same* `q` on maker, hedge, and both exits, each venue's minimum base amount, and each venue's $10 minimum quote amount. If venue steps cease to be nested decimals, compute the true shared lot lattice or reject the route. The old 03:50 HL marks would illustrate about 4.375 NVDA shares or 16.48 oz XAG for a $1,000 maker leg, comfortably above sampled minimums; these are **examples, not live sizes**. ## Predeclared bounded shadow experiment

1. **Freeze before collection:** a timestamped market plan and SHA-256 with all four per-asset venue IDs, lot/minimums, statuses, collateral, HL fee inputs, and published Standard processing times; a method file and its hash; an explicit regular-session versus overnight-session label. Proposed two separate read-only windows of at most **420 seconds and 25 MB compressed each**, one during weekday U.S. regular stock hours and one during a weekday overnight stock session while silver's external market is open. Do not pool these session cohorts. The first window samples ordinary price discovery; the second tests differing overnight oracle inputs. The 5–6 PM ET silver maintenance window, holidays, halts, and weekends need separately labeled research rather than silent inclusion. No credentials or real orders.
2. **Capture:** HL `bbo`, `l2Book`, and `trades` for `xyz:NVDA`/`xyz:SILVER`; Lighter Core and RH ticker, order-book snapshot/deltas, and trade channels for NVDA/XAG. Record source and receipt times, subscription acknowledgements, generation and nonce continuity, quote sizes, unique trade IDs, market/mark/oracle status, and errors. A parseable BBO is one-level depth, never a full executable book. Require one continuous generation per venue; any gap or malformed aggressor/price/size/ID invalidates the affected interval. The RH domain is a separate book and USDG collateral route, not Core liquidity.
3. **Same maker-flow gates as BTC/ETH:** all four maker→hedge routes for **both** assets and both maker sides, fixed UTC 5-second non-overlapping anchors, 1-second assumed maker arrival primary, 0.5 and 3 seconds controls. At decision, both quotes need receipt age ≤1 s, source age ≤2 s, source skew ≤0.5 s, and full `q` displayed at the hedge best. At arrival, the maker-side best price must be unchanged, fresh, and uncrossed; displayed maker top size is modeled queue ahead. From arrival to the next 5-second anchor, opposite-aggressor public trades at or through the maker price must cumulatively **strictly exceed queue ahead + q**. Report every opening quote and full-flow gate, including the predeclared subset with positive opening quote after an approximate four-fee allowance. Public flow is only a hypothetical full-quantity signal, never fill evidence.
4. **Delay-aware hedge:** wait the same baseline **100 ms** after the full-flow trade's receipt. If the hedge taker leg is Core or RH Lighter, add the published **300 ms Standard taker processing** before selecting a book, so the quote-search target is flow receipt + **400 ms**. If the hedge is HL, use flow receipt + 100 ms; HL's actual order processing is still unmeasured. Within the next 1 second, select the *first* time/source/freshness-eligible opposite-side quote whose source is at least the full-flow trade source; **then** test full `q` depth. A shallow first eligible quote censors as unresolved hypothetical unhedged exposure; do not wait for a fuller quote. Store hedge arrival delay and quote age. This is a delayed public-quote screen, not a model of actual order acknowledgement or queue matching.
5. **Delay-aware paired unwind:** target ten seconds after selected hedge receipt. For each taker exit leg, require its own earliest eligible quote after target + 100 ms; add **300 ms** when that leg is on Core or RH Lighter. Form the first observed pair satisfying source/receipt freshness and ≤0.5 s cross-venue skew, then test full `q` on both sides. A shallow first pair censors without a later retry. Record missing hedge, missing/small exit, terminal capture truncation, and clock-source-ahead cases separately; never give them zero P&L. Quote sampling after processing delay is still only a shadow approximation.
6. **Conditional cashflow and controls:** charge four fees on each leg's own entry or exit notional, using the freshly frozen per-asset HL formula and the declared Lighter Standard tier. Report gross, fee net, and net after a **5 bp reserve on the larger entry notional**, for all full-flow signals and the predeclared opening-positive subset. An all-taker same-`q` 10-second control uses the same delayed HL/Lighter books and four own-notional taker fees. Report opening spread and closing basis separately; do not infer P&L from opening spread or take the mean of only complete paths as the value of censored cases. Flag any hourly funding settlement crossed, and report actual signed funding separately if an event occurs. The 0.5/1/3-second scenarios and 10-second holds share frames and are not independent portfolio trades.

## Market risks and stop conditions

- **NVDA:** [XYZ's U.S. stock oracle](https://docs.trade.xyz/perpetuals/markets/stocks/us.md) combines pre-market, regular, post-market, and overnight external sessions; [its internal oracle](https://docs.trade.xyz/perpetuals/mechanics/oracle-price.md) operates when external inputs are absent. [Lighter RWA pricing](https://docs.lighter.xyz/trading/real-world-assets-rwas/rwa-pricing-mechanism.md) can also move from external feeds to internal book-impact pricing when stale. These mechanisms can disagree across a regular close, overnight venue change, or halt. [XYZ corporate-action rules](https://docs.trade.xyz/perpetuals/corporate-actions.md) can settle and reopen positions around splits; dividends are not paid directly to perp holders. Tag oracle mode and reference staleness. Do not mix regular, overnight, closed, or corporate-action intervals or assume a quoted basis will converge in ten seconds.
- **Silver:** [XYZ's SILVER specification](https://docs.trade.xyz/perpetuals/specifications-and-schedules/specification-index.md) references spot XAG/USD per troy ounce, with external coverage Sunday 6 PM–Friday 5 PM ET and daily maintenance. [Core Lighter's XAG specification](https://docs.lighter.xyz/trading/real-world-assets-rwas/market-specifications.md) also uses one ounce but lists Pyth Pro as its oracle. Lighter's [RWA pricing](https://docs.lighter.xyz/trading/real-world-assets-rwas/rwa-pricing-mechanism.md) can use internal prices when external data is stale and states that NVDA/XAG have no fixed caps to the last oracle price. A matching ounce unit does not remove feed, mark, funding, or weekend gap risk.
- **Collateral:** HL/Core use USDC in the sampled plan; RH uses USDG. The RH route needs a contemporaneous executable USDG/USDC conversion and financing/capital assumption before an economic P&L statement. No automatic 1:1 exchange, instant transfer, or cross-margin fungibility is assumed. All three books remain distinct.
- **No-go:** If units, fees, lot/minimums, market status, oracle mode, or collateral route cannot be verified, stop that route. Reject stale/skewed or sequence-broken data and record why. Any full-flow signal without a first full hedge is an unresolved hypothetical exposure. Zero complete paths or zero reserve-positive complete paths is evidence against advancing in the sampled windows; positive cases with few observations require another predeclared shadow window. Even a repeated positive displayed result cannot authorize live orders: actual maker acknowledgment, queue position, adverse selection, processing, transfer, funding, and unwind matching remain unmeasured.

**Review gate:** root should approve or revise this method, market plan, and session schedule *before* adopting a capture. This file is a proposal only; the BTC/ETH archive contains no NVDA or XAG frames and supplies no RWA outcome estimate.
