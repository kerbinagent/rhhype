# Hyperliquid venue survey for Robinhood Chain arbitrage

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-29 03:50 UTC. **Historical window:** 2026-08-29 03:50–2026-09-29 03:50 UTC (744 hourly intervals). This is a venue and hedge inventory, not an executable profit estimate. Source data are the public Hyperliquid `/info` responses in [`data/raw/hyperliquid/20260929T034903Z`](../data/raw/hyperliquid/20260929T034903Z); the reproducible collector is [`scripts/hyperliquid_collect.py`](../scripts/hyperliquid_collect.py).

## Scope and source checks

The snapshot contains **11 perp DEX entries**, including the native DEX: **529 perp listings**, of which **328 were not marked delisted**, and **330 spot pairs**. Exactly 328 perps and 120 spot pairs showed nonzero trailing 24-hour notional volume. Listing counts are not liquidity counts. Of the active perps, 178 are native; 109 are on `xyz`, 29 on `para`, 4 on `mkts`, and 8 on `io`. The other six HIP-3 DEXes were present in metadata but had no active listings at this snapshot. The [official API documentation](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/perpetuals) specifies `perpDexs`, `metaAndAssetCtxs`, funding history, and category endpoints; [spot documentation](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/spot) specifies `spotMetaAndAssetCtxs`.

The spot response has 330 current `universe` entries but 885 asset contexts, so contexts are matched by their **`coin` field**, never by list position. Perp metadata and contexts each have the same length on every DEX in this snapshot and have no context coin identifier, so they are matched by position. Spot display names may differ from API names: `@142` is `UBTC/USDC`, `@151` is `UETH/USDC`, and `@156` is `USOL/USDC`. The [official endpoint guide](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint) explicitly warns of such remapping.

The history sample takes the five highest 24-hour-volume active instruments in each reported category and adds requested tickers. It has **37 perp markets** with raw hourly funding and 1-hour candles, plus [`history_metrics.csv`](../data/raw/hyperliquid/20260929T034903Z/history_metrics.csv). Most established instruments have 744 observations of each kind; newer listings have shorter available histories. The collector paginates at the API's 500-item limit and advances `startTime` by 1 ms after the final timestamp, deduplicating timestamps. [API pagination guidance](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint) supports this. No API errors were recorded. Historic order book depth is unavailable from these candle/funding endpoints; a live executable comparison must use simultaneous order books.

## Cost and margin model

The [official fee schedule](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) gives baseline tier-0 perp taker/maker rates of **0.045% / 0.015%** and spot taker/maker rates of **0.070% / 0.040%** before account-specific discounts, rebates, referral terms, and HIP-3 settings. Many `xyz` equity/index perps show `deployerFeeScale=1.0` and growth mode enabled. Per the official formula, their tier-0 taker fee starts at `0.045% × 2 × 0.1 = 0.009%` before referral and aligned-collateral effects; `xyz:GOLD` has no growth mode in this snapshot, implying `0.090%` at the same tier/scale before further adjustments. These are formula examples, not wallet-specific quotes. Read `userFees` for the actual trader and include **both entry and exit**, spot/token transfer cost, cross-chain bridge/redemption charges, and expected funding over holding time. A fee scale of 1.0 is stored per **asset**, not per DEX.

HIP-3 DEXes may have different collateral tokens, so the inventory resolves `collateralToken` indices to spot token names. Active `xyz`, `para`, `mkts`, `io`, and native markets here use USDC. Inactive DEX metadata includes USDH, USDE, and USDT0 collateral. The [HIP-3 specification](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hyperliquid-improvement-proposals-hips/hip-3-builder-deployed-perpetuals) says each deployed DEX has separate margining and order books, and deployers define and operate their own oracle and contract specifications. A same-name contract on another DEX is therefore not fungible by assumption. Native perps are generally linear, no-expiry, hourly funded contracts, with USDC margin and some USDT-denominated oracle exposure as described in [contract specifications](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/contract-specifications). The funding payment uses oracle price, not mark price.

For a Robinhood Chain long token hedged by a Hyperliquid short perp, a conservative executable edge at quantity `q` is:

`(Hyperliquid sell proceeds at bid depth − Robinhood Chain buy cost at ask depth) / Robinhood Chain buy cost`

Then subtract both venues' entry and exit fees, gas, transfer/bridge/redemption charges, expected adverse execution, funding owed (or add funding received), collateral financing, and a reserve for oracle/corporate-action basis. Reverse the bid/ask and funding sign for the opposite direction. Require both books to be sampled near the same time, size through depth rather than at the top quote, and reject stale or halted markets. The hourly candles are useful for volatility and persistent basis study; they cannot establish that an arbitrage was executable.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `python scripts/hyperliquid_collect.py --census-only` for a timestamped full census, then `python scripts/hyperliquid_collect.py --history-only --run-dir data/raw/hyperliquid/<timestamp> --days 31` for history. A completed run can be resumed without refetching existing raw pages. The script uses only Python standard library and public read-only `/info` requests. The [official rate-limit page](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/rate-limits-and-user-limits) documents a 1,200-weight-per-minute REST limit and response-size weights for funding and candles; the collector retries transient failures and records persistent errors.

This is a 31-day observational sample from one point-in-time market inventory. It does not capture historic spreads or depth, actual fill probability, Robinhood Chain prices, cross-chain settlement times, wallet-specific fee tiers, or stock-token rights. New listings such as `xyz:OURA`, `io:EWY`, and `para:2Y`/`30Y` have less than 30 days of available observations; [`history_summary.json`](../data/raw/hyperliquid/20260929T034903Z/history_summary.json) states exact coverage. Cross-venue conclusions require those remaining measurements.
