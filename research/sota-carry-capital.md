# Research direction: cross-venue carry with explicit capital and basis risk

**29 September 2026. Research proposal only; no orders or production-policy change.** The immediate priority is the user's small RH maker bid/HL hedge hypothesis; the funding-carry work below is a separate, slower-horizon research path. See the [strategy synthesis](sota-strategy-review.md) for the near-term decision. “State of the art” here means a serious, testable research design grounded in published work and current venue rules. Private arbitrage desks' forecasts, fills, fee tiers and returns are unknown. An apparent funding spread is a risky position with no fixed convergence date, not an assured arbitrage: [He, Manela, Ross and von Wachter (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888) derive cost-dependent no-arbitrage bounds and explicitly describe margin and liquidation risk. [Schmeling, Schrimpf and Todorov, *Crypto Carry* (2026)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05069) find that carry varies with leveraged demand and scarce arbitrage capital; their empirical BTC/ETH futures result is motivation, not evidence of profit on our venues.

## Trade the economics of a position, not the opening spread alone

For one matched unit, short the richer perp venue **S** and long venue **L**. Define the executable basis $B_t=P^S_t-P^L_t$, using sell-side depth on S and buy-side depth on L at entry, and the opposite sides on exit. Over a chosen holding period $t\to T$, the dollar result is

\[
\Pi_{t,T}=q(B_t-B_T)
+\sum_{\tau\in(t,T]}q\{I^S_\tau f^S_\tau-I^L_\tau f^L_\tau\}
-C_{\rm four\ fills}-C_{\rm collateral}-C_{\rm rebalance}-C_{\rm capital}.
\]

Here $f>0$ means longs pay shorts, and each venue's own settlement index $I$ converts the signed rate to dollars. Equal base quantity $q$ hedges first-order asset exposure; it does **not** hedge basis, index/oracle divergence, stablecoin conversion or liquidation. Use actual quantities, contract multipliers and settled rates in accounting. At decision time, $B_T$ and future rates are unknown: estimate their *distribution from earlier data*, then stress adverse tails. A funding rate displayed now must not be carried through an entire multi-day hold as if fixed. [Hyperliquid's funding specification](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding) pays hourly, uses the oracle price for payment notional and computes the premium from impact prices. [Lighter's funding specification](https://docs.lighter.xyz/trading/funding) likewise pays hourly, but uses its own minute-sampled premium, market multiplier and index. These structural differences make a predictive differential worth testing, while also making raw displayed rates an imperfect cross-venue comparison.

The first candidate is **perp-versus-perp carry** on matched crypto underlyings across Hyperliquid, Core Lighter and Robinhood Chain Lighter: collect the more favorable signed funding while holding a balanced hedge. BTC, ETH and SOL are cleaner starting controls than ticker-matched RWA perps; XAG/NVDA need separate reference and session-risk review. A second candidate is **spot-versus-perp cash-and-carry** only where a public, actually executable spot or borrow path and collateral rules can be represented. The existing perp monitor does not establish that path. Avoid assuming a short spot position can be borrowed cheaply or that spot and perp balances share margin.

Candidate decisions are: which route and direction; whether the prospective funding more than compensates the executable opening and expected closing basis; position size; and whether to continue, rebalance or close after each new rate/price observation. An implementable conservative entry score is a *lower bound* on expected dollar P&L over a prechosen horizon after all four taker fills, pessimistic exit depth, negative funding surprises and capital cost. Enter only when that bound is positive **and** each prefunded venue wallet retains a liquidation buffer. Close when the remaining expected carry no longer pays for basis/margin risk, when a predeclared drawdown or holding limit is reached, or when the hedge or data fails. This makes an hourly-to-multiday holding rule an empirical choice; ten seconds has no special economic connection to hourly funding. Just-before-settlement entry is especially vulnerable to the basis adjustment around funding: He et al. report that a five-minute-before/five-minute-after funding trade's gross effect was attenuated by price moves and trading costs in their older Binance sample. That observation does not identify our venues' current response.

## Capital is a route constraint

At the current monitor's $1,000 per leg and four prefunded wallets, a strategy needs cash and margin on both venues before the apparent spread exists. Score a route by expected *risk-adjusted dollars per scarce venue dollar-hour*, not annualized funding alone. Allocate across routes subject to wallet cash, initial/maintenance margin plus a stress buffer, concentration by asset/venue, and limits on funding or basis losses. Apply haircuts to positive uncollected funding; reserve known debits. Model rebalancing or bridge transfers as delayed, priced actions, not instantaneous free capital. [Robinhood's own wallet documentation](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-wallet-perpetual-futures/) says its Lighter position uses USDG margin locked on Robinhood Chain; closed collateral returns to a trading-contract balance before withdrawal. Core/HL collateral and RH USDG therefore cannot be treated as one immediately fungible wallet. A positive opportunity on RH can be unusable if the RH wallet is empty, regardless of total portfolio cash. Funding can change sign, collateral can move relative to USD, and one leg can liquidate before the long-run carry is earned.

Inventory controls should prefer natural offsetting flows or cheap internal reallocations. Rebalance only when mismatch and risk saved exceed the extra spread, fees, delay and possible one-leg exposure. Compare marginal uses of the same constrained wallet: a route promising more annualized funding may earn less after tying up collateral longer or forcing a costly transfer. This is an optimization hypothesis to test, not an assertion that our public monitor can observe a private desk's optimal inventory.

**RH small-maker hypothesis.** The new RH venue may leave room for small passive bids/offers that are hedged on HL after a *verified* RH fill, then held until a favorable net unwind or a risk/age stop. Test $25/$50/$100/$250 per leg separately: smaller size may face less depth impact but minimum sizes, queue position, partial fills, HL hedge fees and collateral still matter. Recycling an RH position against later natural opposite flow may avoid one unwind; it must remain a dated inventory position with mark, funding, margin and an eventual exit liability, never book the entry spread as profit. A fixed ten-second close could destroy the economics even if a slower exit works. The old $0.10 **absolute** net target equals 40/20/10/4 bp on $25/$50/$100/$250 respectively before considering the two-leg cash tied up; compare net basis points and risk-adjusted dollars per venue dollar-hour, while retaining the live monitor's $0.10 rule unchanged. The prior maker capture has no RH fill proof, so a public-flow replay remains conditional.

## What our evidence does and does not say

- The [frozen 48-hour funding screen](../reports/funding-carry/REPORT.md) selected directions on 24 hours and evaluated 24 held-out settlements for 16 HL–Core/RH pairs. The best held-out funding advantage was 4.52 bp (SOL/RH), below its 9 bp four-Standard-taker-fee hurdle before spread/impact, plus the modeled 5 bp extra cost. **It rejects a 24-hour, funding-only, four-taker-fee justification in that small window.** It did not measure a longer forecasted hold, dynamic exit basis, spot carry, maker fills or an actually funded portfolio. Its 74-hour break-even extrapolation is descriptive, not a forecast.
- The [frozen fee sensitivity](../reports/fee-sensitivity/REPORT.md) kept 527 short-hold paper closes and their prices/exits fixed: recorded net was −$624.27; removing all explicit trading fees *and* the modeled reserve still gave −$228.90. This rejects “cheaper fees alone fix these same ten-second fills.” It says nothing about a new multi-hour entry/exit policy.
- The [prospective maker quote study](maker-equity-results.md) produced one hypothetical full-flow signal but no complete exit quote pair in a seven-minute post-market capture. It gives no realized maker P&L or fill probability. The [impulse pilot](../reports/impulse-v1/final.md) had zero confirmed candidates under its frozen ten-second gates. Neither falsifies carry, nor warrants assigning censored observations a zero return.

## Predeclared public-data test, with no orders

1. **Near-term feasibility from data already available.** First review the archived RH/HL session quotes, public trades and the short settled-funding sample for $25/$50/$100/$250 RH passive sizes, minimum-order compliance, displayed queue, later HL hedge depth and eventual exit coverage. Record how often a maker quote has *conditional* public-flow support and how long inventory would remain. RH Chain is new, so earlier RH market history may not exist; pre-inception hours stay missing, never backfilled or treated as zero. This screen can reject an infeasible rule or choose what to collect next, but it cannot certify fills or expected returns.
2. **Freeze separate prospective tests.** Make RH small-maker/HL hedge the primary near-term hypothesis. Freeze each size, queue/partial-fill rule, hedge timing, capital limit, financing, inventory-recycling rule and terminal exit charge before observing new outcomes. A $1,000-per-leg taker carry study across BTC/ETH/SOL HL–Core/RH is a separate secondary cohort with Standard four-taker fees, Premium and 5/10 bp cost sensitivities and a 48-hour cap. RWA routes need a separate reference review. Do not select only favorable symbols afterward.
3. **Long validation phase, when enough RH history exists.** Collect up to 60 consecutive prospective days of timestamped, source-valid, size-specific public depth; public trades; index/oracle and current predicted funding inputs; settled funding; and feed gaps. Use native streaming, bounded depth checks and independent receipt timestamps. If 60 comparable days accrue, use 30 for fitting, 15 for choosing one threshold and holding rule, and 15 untouched for validation. This is a validation design, not a promise of an immediate 60-day result; report incomplete sample coverage explicitly. The taker carry cohort can compare a 24-hour funding baseline with an hourly updated 48-hour policy. Keep the user's live ten-second policy intact and report it separately.
4. **Replay in timestamp order.** At an eligible decision, freeze route, side and quantity; require both leg entry quotes *after* modeled processing/receipt delay, walk available depth once per shared portfolio, and use later eligible opposite-side exit quotes. For RH maker, require price persistence plus strict public opposite-side flow exceeding displayed queue ahead and size before allowing a **conditional** fill; hedge later on HL and retain inventory until a priced exit. Mark missing depth, stale source, disconnected periods, unobserved future settlements, unmatched hedge and open-at-end positions as censored or exposed, never as wins or zeros. Charge actual observed funding only at crossed settlements, explicit fill fees, margin financing, and USDG/USDC conversion or an adverse sensitivity where no executable conversion quote exists. Keep separate venue wallets and stress one-leg/forced-liquidation scenarios. Public quotes cannot establish actual order acceptance, maker queue priority or slippage under our own order flow.
5. **Report decision criteria on the untouched holdout:** complete paired net dollars and return per venue dollar-hour; count and economic size of censored cases; worst basis excursion and margin shortfall; rate forecast error; funding versus basis versus fees versus rebalancing contribution; and drawdown/stress losses. Bootstrap by day (not by repeated quote) with route clustering, and show the full cost sensitivities. A candidate advances only if the predeclared holdout has positive net after main costs *and* its conservative uncertainty bound is above zero, with no hidden margin deficit or material unresolved cashflow. Otherwise retain the negative result and revise the hypothesis on a new future sample.

**Recommendation:** test the user's small RH maker bid with a later time-eligible HL hedge quote and eventual inventory unwind first, beginning with an archived-data feasibility audit and then frozen prospective observation. This preserves the current live ten-second monitor and its $0.10 target. Study slower funding carry separately once enough data accrue: it asks whether a realizable funding differential and managed basis can pay for execution and immobilized collateral, a different holding objective from the ten-second policy.
