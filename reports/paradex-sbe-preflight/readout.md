# Paradex public streaming preflight

One unauthenticated 60-second connection completed without errors. Four subscription acknowledgements; 194 full 15-level book snapshots and 24 funding messages. An independent binary-layout audit agrees with the collector. All raw frames and the official XML schema are retained.

193/194 book messages were within two seconds of their published timestamps. BTC median age was 0.177 seconds; ETH median 0.162 seconds. One initial ETH snapshot was 8.015 seconds old and is unusable for execution. Freshness must be checked on each later event too. Funding messages were about 0.56 seconds old and arrived approximately every five seconds.

This resolves the transport obstacle found in the REST preflight. It does not establish an edge or simulate any trade. Further trading tests need actual venue identity, retail/API fee distinction, 400 ms retail order model, continuous funding index accounting and fresh delayed books. Published timestamps do not authenticate matching-engine acceptance.
