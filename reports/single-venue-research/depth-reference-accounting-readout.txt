fill residual = actual local gross - reference gross

This compares actual fills to frictionless reference-midpoint exposure.
The reference component is neither an executable hedge nor a causal estimate.

actual cash stays in its native collateral. The residual mixes relative price
movement and local execution costs. No automatic strategy-profit claim.
