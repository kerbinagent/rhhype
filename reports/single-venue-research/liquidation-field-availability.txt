mixed types and suppressed10 market/generation streams afterward. Original
inventory v1 retained18261 of25720 ordinary prints, losing7459 after those
rejections. The raw capture is intact; repair uses explicit subtype exclusion.

ordinary-trade-only count. Do not sum both arrays as separate executions.

Mechanism interpretation: positive public order IDs and liquidation subtype
are consistent with documented partial-liquidation reduce-only IOC orders.
They do not prove every wire serialization boundary or that every row consumed
displayed liquidity. Full takeover and ADL have distinct mechanisms. This
one-sided clustered sample only establishes availability; ordinary-flow controls,
common-market effects, delayed costs and later untouched data remain necessary
before any profitability claim. No liquidation recovery model was fitted.
