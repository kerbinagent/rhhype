BID/ASK ANATOMY OF EARLIER DEPTH SHOCKS

Retrospective check of all 24 admitted signal rows: 13 distinct asset/venue/
print/decision opportunities after deduplicating paired follow/fade alternatives.
This includes entries that did not fill. It excludes non-admitted detector events.
No current three-window batch data was inspected.

In 10 of 13 opportunities, less than half of the combined bid/ask movement was
on the side used to enter the fade. Several midpoint jumps mainly widened the
spread. For the latest Core LIT winner, the bid moved 3.8324 -> 3.8325 while the
ask moved 3.8334 -> 3.8364. The entry-side bid supplied only 3.23% of that combined
quote movement. Its signal-time bid was below the reference midpoint 3.83355.
This does not negate the later audited cash gain; it changes its interpretation.

This optimistic quote comparison omits size/depth, order delay, fees and exit
spread. The reference midpoint is not established fair value; venue basis and
collateral parity also matter. A positive value is not an executable profit.
Potential future tests: detect movement in the executable side, distinguish
spread widening from a shifted book, and compare a wider entry IOC allowance.
These are new hypotheses, not changes to the active frozen batch or evidence
that a different entry rule would be profitable.
