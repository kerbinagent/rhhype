Public position-field availability — economics-free preparation

Question: does the existing raw ordinary-trade archive contain enough explicit
pre-fill position information to consider a past-only conditional-value feature?
This audit calculates no closing/opening classifier, future price return, fill,
execution cost or cash result. No account IDs were used as grouping keys, queried,
printed or retained in derived output.

Decision: field availability passes. Treat signed base-unit, per-fill position
state as an empirically supported interpretation in these samples, not a normative
venue guarantee. Register the interpretation and failure cases before testing
predictive value. Missing optional fields must never be converted to zero.
A later fill's class cannot retroactively determine whether to post an earlier
passive order. Position state does not reveal trader intent, remaining parent
orders, liquidation proximity, or our fill accessibility.

Method: Sol independently verified the two raw/manifest/normalized-metadata pins
from single-venue-directional-response-v1.json before and after bounded streaming.

and liquidation/deleverage types were excluded. Bounds were1GiB decoded,

Chronology finding: multi-fill trade arrays are reverse ordered. Raw array order
produced false position-arithmetic mismatches. Sorting observed taker-order rows
within each message by(transaction_time,trade_id) produced:
next_position_before = current_position_before + signed_current_fill_quantity
for2364/2364 adjacentpairs across948 multi-fillmessage/ordergroups inW1 and
2128/2128 across971groups inW2. Buyquantity is positive, sellquantity negative.
No multi-fill group shared one constant priorposition.

Across whole observed takerorders,2369/2369W1 and2136/2137W2 adjacentpairs matched.
The lone cross-message exception remains unexplained; orderID alone does not
prove uninterrupted ownership/version or all account activity. Do not repair it
by inventing hidden trades or assuming the optional flag is false.
The(order,tx_hash,transaction_time,block_height) versionproxy made everygroup a
singleton, because transaction_time differed perprint. It is not a useful proxy
for a multi-fill order version in these captures.

Current official schema lists the optional position strings and sign-change
booleans and shows negative maker position values. It does not clearly define
per-fill versus order-level semantics in normative prose. The arithmetic above
supports our sample-specific interpretation.

Hypothesis to consider next, not an empirical finding: recent risk reduction may
predict less persistent adverse flow than recent same-direction position adds.
Closing traders can also be informed. Test a strictly past-only classifier with
asset/side/spread/queue/price-move/flow-size controls and realistic delayed entry
before another passive execution branch. Count missing fields, reversals,
non-fills, partialfills and missing exits; require positive conditional closed
value as well as comparative improvement. These preparation counts do not show
predictive value or paperprofit.
