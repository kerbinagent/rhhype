1. Cross-asset response after a BTC move

Mechanism hypothesis: systematic traders or market makers may adjust alt
exposure after BTC moves, leaving part of the directional response after our
entry delay. This is a behavioral inference. It does not imply causation, a
stable beta or a one-for-one catch-up price. It differs from same-asset
cross-venue convergence, while sharing its serious latency/cost obstacles.

Kurihara and Matsumoto study minute-price BTC-to-alt relationships on Binance,
with small illiquid assets outside this ten-asset cohort. That evidence does
not establish executable delayed Core returns. Huth and Abergel explicitly
find useful high-frequency prediction whose naive market-order value is
eliminated by spread. These are motivation and an adverse prior, respectively.

2. Post-funding position unwind — parked

Hypothesis: after positive funding, recipient shorts buy to close and paying
longs rebuild exposure, creating upward pressure; reverse for negative funding.
This behavioral prediction is materially different from constant carry. Core
and RH document hourly margin transfers, but payment requires no book trade
or deterministic price reset. The rate averages prior premium observations;
the terminal book premium is not the settled rate. A funding receipt alone is
not profit when the price move and exit costs are included.
[Core funding](https://docs.lighter.xyz/trading/funding)
[RH funding and settlement](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/robinhood-wallet-perpetual-futures/)

The current rolling source subscribes only to order_book/trade and performs
four startup metadata requests. It lacks timestamped pre-boundary upcoming
funding estimates and observed settlement updates. Official market_stats
separates current_funding_rate from the previous funding_rate/timestamp, so
joining a settled rate afterward would introduce hindsight into this signal.
Startup metadata cannot establish boundary paths of index, premium or open
interest. Adjacent capture chunks also do not guarantee continuous coverage.
These are source-level availability findings, not a raw-data audit.

If the missing observations later become authorized, the fixed diagnostic
would use the estimate received byT-5s, enter in its sign after confirmed

fees. Credit zero funding only when the confirmed payment precedes entry and
