# Paired passive RH hedge-venue diagnostic

Run `python scripts/analyze_passive_hedge_venues.py --out reports/passive-hedge-venues` from the repository root. The script reads only two complete, stopped archives: BTC/ETH at 2026-09-29 **19:39–19:46 UTC** and NVDA/XAG at **20:22–20:29 UTC**. It verifies the SHA-256 of each pre-capture market plan against its archive manifest, makes no network requests, and writes at most 10,000 paired rows. The current output has **4,336** rows: two RH directions × four sizes × **274 static + 268 delayed** common anchors. The `source_sha256` object in [summary.json](summary.json) identifies both inputs, market plans, and the analyzer.

[matched-rows.csv](matched-rows.csv) records exact common quantity, entry and exit quotes, each hedge route's four leg notionals, explicit fees, capital cost, separate 5 bp stress allowance, and target flags. Static rows are conditional same-book spread arithmetic. Delayed rows use the first matched +10–16 s quote whose three source and receipt timestamps are all after +10 s. Neither type records real passive fills or latency-adjusted execution. The [research note](../../research/passive-hedge-venue-followup.md) explains the economics, official fee sources, coverage, and practical conclusion.

There were no size-invalid or hedge-depth-missing rows among matched cases. Missing rows remain in each group's coverage counters in `summary.json`.
