# RH maker / Core hedge ETH: inconclusive public execution study

Frozen four independent branches: $100/$1,000 per leg, passive exit or taker exit after ten seconds. Capture stopped at its predeclared 1,048,576-byte cap after 305.445 seconds. No parameter changes or extensions.

Every branch attempted three quotes. Two retired with no qualifying trade flow. The third became UNKNOWN: qualifying trade flow arrived before the first book whose source time passed the 400 ms activation deadline. Queue placement cannot be established from this archive. The branch stopped opening quotes at that point.

There were no modeled fills or completed trades. Cash sums of zero are not strategy returns; complete branch P&L is null and the third quote's possible exposure remains unknown. The independent audit passed bookkeeping, hashes and unknown-state retention, but had no fills to validate.

This sample cannot establish profitability. Its useful finding is that strict public queue reconstruction censored the maker experiment almost immediately. A follow-up will broaden the short-term taker basis study, preserving delayed execution and recorded candidate books.
