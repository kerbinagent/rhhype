# Frozen exploratory all-taker size diagnostic

**Design status:** This size screen was chosen after seeing the NVDA/XAG maker replay's sparse full-flow and missing exit. It is an exploratory post hoc diagnostic of the same stopped 2026-09-29 20:22 UTC archive, not an independent holdout or executable trade test. Freeze this file before running the size analyzer. No orders, authentication, new captures, or changes to the earlier frozen maker result.

## Universe and anchors

- Assets NVDA and XAG, the frozen HL/Core/RH market IDs and original common lots from `reports/maker-equity-v2/market-plan.json`, tier-0 fee inputs from `reports/maker-equity-v2/fee-inputs.json`.
- Four directed routes: buy HL/sell Core, buy Core/sell HL, buy HL/sell RH, buy RH/sell HL. Three fixed target sizes: **$100, $250, $1,000**. All 83 UTC five-second anchors from the earlier capture, for 1,992 asset/route/size observations in total. The same anchors and frames recur across sizes; results cannot be summed as portfolio trades.
- At each anchor, require both venue quotes with receipt age ≤1 s and source age ≤2 s, and source and receipt skew each ≤0.5 s. These are the frozen earlier decision clock gates. Size `q` once as `floor(target USD / fresh buy-venue best ask / common_lot) × common_lot` using decimal arithmetic. Check both venues' frozen lot, minimum, maximum, and $10 minimum quote at their **anchor** entry-side prices. Check every later leg again at its own first eligible displayed price. No dynamic resize, learned direction or filter, or later price selection. The buy leg's displayed anchor notional is at most its target; the sell leg may differ and is reported.

## Independently delayed entry and exit

- Both entry taker requests are modeled at the anchor. HL's quote due time is anchor **+100 ms**; Core/RH Lighter Standard's is anchor **+400 ms** (100 ms baseline plus 300 ms published taker processing). For each venue independently take the **first** quote with both source and receipt ≥ its due time, receipt-minus-source age 0–2 s, and receipt within due +1 s. Then test the original `q` against that quote's opposing top size and venue minimum/maximum. If this first quote is shallow or below minimum, classify that leg unresolved and never retry at later depth. If one leg passes and the other does not, explicitly report a one-leg unresolved hypothetical outcome; do not score a paired entry or silently drop it.
- Only when both entry legs pass, request exits **5 seconds after the later entry quote receipt**. HL and Core/RH exit quotes become due after their same +100/+400 ms processing delays. For each venue independently select its first source-and-receipt eligible quote within due +1 s and before the **hard deadline of anchor +10 seconds**. Test the original `q` against the correct closing top side and market minimum/maximum at that first quote. A missing or shallow counterpart is unresolved, preserving any independently observed first leg. A capture-end truncation is separately labeled when the effective search window extends beyond capture end. There is no retry, fill interpolation, or reliance on deeper L2 levels. A simultaneous cross-venue quote-pair skew requirement is not imposed on these independently delayed actions; record actual intervenue source and receipt skew on complete paths.
- If an hourly UTC funding boundary falls from the first entry quote through the last exit quote, classify funding unresolved and omit net. Missing legs are never assigned zero return.

## Arithmetic and reporting

- A complete conditional quote path uses fixed `q`: buy entry at ask, sell entry at bid, buy-venue exit at bid, sell-venue exit at ask. Gross is `q[(buy exit bid − buy entry ask) + (sell entry bid − sell exit ask)]`. Charge all four tier-0 **taker** fees on their own leg notionals: HL 0.9 bp, Core/RH Standard 0 bp under the frozen public inputs. Report separately a 5 bp reserve on the larger entry notional and illustrative capital cost at 5% annual simple rate on **100% of each entry notional for that leg's observed entry-to-exit quote interval**, matching the paper engine's default margin fraction. Funding, conversion execution, and actual order matching are not included. RH USDG/USDC comparison conditionally assumes parity only for displayed-quote arithmetic.
- Report gate counts by size, asset, and directed route, preserving missing/shallow first-leg and one-leg outcomes. For complete nonfunding paths, report distributions for each size **and** the intersection of the exact asset/route/anchor keys complete at all three sizes. Compare size-specific coverage with this matched-anchor intersection; do not read larger/smaller-size differences as strategy performance when coverage differs. Display gross, four-fee, reserve, and illustrative capital separately. Zero complete paths is a valid result.
- Public source timestamps and local receipts are not synchronized execution or measured network latency. A selected public top size does not imply a fill; the model has no queue position, order acknowledgement, slippage, financing quote, or collateral transfer. This diagnostic cannot establish actual profit, a mathematical P&L bound, or future/regular-hours performance.
