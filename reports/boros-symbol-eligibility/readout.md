# Funding-swap eligibility — 1 October 2026

The public registry includes `lighter-eth` (ETH, exchange Lighter). This is
a lead for fixing Core ETH funding receipts. Registration does not prove a
live tradable maturity, enough depth, a favorable rate, or exact settlement
and currency matching. No quote was obtained.

A short rate swap receives fixed and pays floating. Its floating leg must
match Core funding ownership, quantity, timing and denomination. Separate
swap collateral adds capital and liquidation risk; a coin-denominated fixed
rate is not a fixed dollar return. Entry, settlement and entrance fees apply.
See official [settlement](https://docs.pendle.finance/boros-docs/about-boros/funding-rate-settlement)
and [fees](https://docs.pendle.finance/boros-docs/boros-systems/fees).
