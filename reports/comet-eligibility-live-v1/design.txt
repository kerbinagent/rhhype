magic/frame/body-fragment append is fsynced. B begins exact request/name/timeout/
clocks; H contains HTTP status/full parsed header list; D is the exact received
body fragment; E records status/body bytes/SHA256/EOF/error/end clocks. Full RPC
bodies, including complete block responses and provider errors, remain untouched.
No base64 export, gzip duplicate, normalized raw response or second header file.
Frames have <=8192B payload, total655360B. Header<=8192B. Each response<=65536B,
with at most the engine's counted65537B sentinel, cumulative body<=524288B. Every
read1 limit also respects remaining raw space and a4096B end-frame reserve; it
