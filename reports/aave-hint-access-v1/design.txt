Post-hoc continuation of the admitted health/price crossing. Is this exact
prefix identifiable in a fixed public historical-hint subset, and what

The official Flashbots event-stream documentation describes historical hints
at /api/v1/history, with /info metadata, block/time filters, limit and offset.
Hint root hashes can be keccak256(transaction hash) or bundle hashes; an
optional txs[].hash identifies an underlying transaction. History block/time
labels describe the provider's view when it generated the hint. Fields may
be withheld. Mainnet host is mev-share.flashbots.net.
https://docs.flashbots.net/flashbots-mev-share/searchers/event-stream

Exactly two fixed GET slots, no retries/fallbacks/redirects/streaming:
1 /api/v1/history/info,4096B body. Required count/minBlock/maxBlock/
minTimestamp/maxTimestamp/maxLimit integer fields. Reported coverage must
include the complete fixed range and maxLimit must allow100, otherwise stop.
2 /api/v1/history?blockStart=26103136&blockEnd=26103141&limit=100&offset=0,
262144B body. Fixed six-block range ending at the original event, first100
returned hints only. Never paginate, extend time or repeat on an empty sample.

Match categories are distinct: underlying tx hash or documented root double
hash establishes a hash identity match within provider data. Exact recipient
plus complete calldata is a content match, weaker than transaction identity.
Missing fields stay missing. Preserve all returned raw hints and report every
match; counts include duplicate rows, not independent opportunities.
No match means only absence from this returned subset. Even fewer than100
rows does not establish archive completeness. Exactly100 marks the page limit.
Returned rows outside the range or malformed schemas invalidate the study.

For matches retain supplied fields, timestamp, provider head block and target
block timestamp minus hint timestamp. This difference is a provider-label
comparison, not local network arrival or measured actionable lead time.
Hash/calldata matches do not prove the historical winning auction, builder
delivery, our ability to execute, net profit or repeatability. No signing,
bundle submission, funded account, contract deployment or trading occurs.
